Before you join

Frequently asked

Is this just another job board?

No. It doesn't compete on how many listings it has; it competes on judging each one against criteria you actually wrote, the way a recruiter working for you would.

What do I need to set it up?

A CV and ten minutes to write your brief: what you do, what you want, what you won't accept. It reads the CV so you don't fill in a profile.

Does it only cover games?

Yes, deliberately. It's tuned for game-industry studios, engines and hiring habits — not a generic filter with a genre checkbox.

Which disciplines?

Any game-industry role: engineering, art, design, production, audio, QA. It judges against your brief, not a fixed list of job titles.

Where does it look?

Job boards, studio careers pages and the game-industry sources it can reach. Some networks keep their listings behind a login, and those it can't read. Each alert says where the posting was found.

How fast will I hear about a posting?

As soon as it has found and judged it — it checks its sources continuously, not once a week. It can't promise a number of days, and a posting it can't reach, it can't see.

What if I get too many messages, or too few?

Tell it how often you want to hear from it and it moves the bar. Still off? Tighten or loosen your brief: everything it found is there to browse, including what it held back and why, and the whole set is re-judged on the spot.

What if it gets something wrong?

It shows its work — the score and the verdict on every criterion — so you can see exactly why, disagree, and tell it so with one tap. If it keeps missing the same way, that's a line to add to your brief.

Does it get me hired?

No. It finds and judges roles; applying, interviewing and getting the job are still yours. It doesn't promise a placement rate, because it can't.

Am I the only one seeing these leads?

No. Anyone with a similar brief sees overlapping leads; there is no exclusivity to sell. The edge is being early, and being judged against your own brief rather than a keyword.

What happens to my CV?

It's read to build your brief and kept to judge postings against you. It's never sold, traded or handed to a third party — the one exception is the AI that runs the hunt, which reads it for that purpose only. No studio ever sees it.

Which apps does it message?

Telegram at launch. Discord support is planned. If neither works for you, email is the fallback.
